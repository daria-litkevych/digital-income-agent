# ScopeSnap — Draft Listing (Not Published)

## Product name
ScopeSnap — Scope Change Estimator for Freelancers

## Short promise
Turn an extra client request into a clear, priced change summary before you start the extra work.

## Description
A lightweight browser tool for freelancers and independent service providers who want to handle out-of-scope requests more clearly.

Enter the original agreement, the client's new request, your estimated extra hours, hourly rate and expenses. ScopeSnap calculates the additional amount and prepares a client-ready summary you can copy or print as a PDF.

### What is included
- Instant additional-cost calculation
- Transparent breakdown of hours, rate and extra expenses
- Timeline-impact field
- Client-ready change summary
- Copy and print / save as PDF
- No account, no subscription and no external integrations in the current version

### How it works
1. Open the HTML file in a modern browser.
2. Describe the original scope and the new request.
3. Enter the estimated time and costs.
4. Review the generated summary.
5. Copy it into your preferred communication channel or save it as a PDF.

### Privacy
The prototype runs locally in the browser. It does not upload or save entered data between sessions. Do not enter confidential client information in a test version.

### Important
ScopeSnap is a communication aid. It does not interpret contracts, determine legal rights, provide legal advice, collect signatures or guarantee that a change is contractually binding. Review the wording and terms yourself before sending.
