# ScopeSnap — Validation plan

## Hypothesis
Freelancers will find it useful to turn an extra client request into a priced, clear change summary before starting the work.

## Test participants
5–8 freelancers or independent service providers who have handled fixed-scope projects.

## Task
Give the participant a realistic scenario: a client asks for an additional deliverable after the original scope was agreed. Ask them to use ScopeSnap without a walkthrough.

## Observe
- Can they understand what to enter?
- Can they produce a usable summary without help?
- Does the calculation match their expectations?
- Would they actually send the summary to a client?
- What do they use today (email, contract clause, template, CRM, project software)?
- What feels missing or too formal?
- Would they pay for any part of this, and what specifically?

## Success signals (directional, not statistical)
- At least 4 of 5 participants complete the task without help.
- At least 3 say they would use/send the output in a real project.
- At least 2 describe a current recurring workaround or financial consequence.
- At least 2 express credible interest in a paid enhancement at a stated price.

## Stop / revise signals
- Participants prefer a simple email template or existing contract clause.
- The tool adds more friction than it removes.
- The output is not trusted or does not fit their client relationships.
- No one would use it more than once.

## Guardrails
No cold outreach, paid ads, paid API or public launch is part of this test without separate approval. Do not ask participants to upload contracts or client-sensitive information.
