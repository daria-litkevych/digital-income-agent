# ScopeSnap — Facilitator Test Script

**Purpose:** Test whether the prototype removes friction from preparing a scope-change proposal. This is usability research, not a sales pitch.

## Before the session
- Ask permission to take notes. Do not record audio/video unless separately agreed.
- Do not request real client names, contracts, emails, invoices or confidential project details.
- Use the fictional scenario below.
- Tell the participant the prototype is experimental and free during testing.

## Opening (30 seconds)
"We're testing a small tool for freelancers. It helps prepare a clear estimate when a client asks for extra work. I'm interested in what is confusing or unnecessary; there are no right answers."

## Scenario
"You agreed to design a five-page website for €1,500, including two rounds of consolidated revisions. The client now asks you to add a blog page and one extra revision round. You estimate 3.5 additional hours at €60/hour and €15 in extra expenses. The change will add two business days."

## Task
"Use the tool to prepare a summary you could send to the client. Please think aloud as you go."

Do not explain fields unless the participant is stuck for 30 seconds; note where help was needed.

## Questions after the task
1. What did you expect the tool to do that it did not do?
2. Would you send this summary to a real client? Why or why not?
3. How do you handle extra requests today?
4. What part of the current process is most frustrating?
5. How often does this happen?
6. What does a typical mistake or delay cost you?
7. Would you rather use a template, calculator, client approval link or a feature inside your existing software?
8. Would you pay for this? If yes, what exact feature would justify payment and what price feels reasonable?

## Record
For each session, capture:
- Participant type (e.g. designer, developer, consultant; no identifying details)
- Completion time
- Completed without help? (yes/no)
- Would send output? (yes/no/unsure)
- Current workaround
- Most useful feature
- Main objection
- Willingness-to-pay statement (verbatim where possible)
- Follow-up suggestion

## Interpretation
Five to eight sessions are directional only. Do not generalise percentages to the whole freelancer market. Look for repeated patterns and actual behaviour, not compliments.
