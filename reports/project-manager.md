# Project Manager — Online Passive Income

**Research update:** 2026-11-12

## Current status

- Competitor mapping and pricing comparison are complete for both initial hypotheses.
- Qualitative customer-pain scan has been expanded with three additional community signals: café staffing/stock/waste and hotel shift-handover failures.
- The evidence is still anecdotal. It does not establish market size, frequency across the sector or willingness to pay.
- **Next task:** compare the two hypotheses and define the cheapest validation experiment. Do not build a full product yet.

## Evidence-based observations

1. Generic café checklists are weakly differentiated because free specialty-coffee alternatives and cheap paid templates already exist.
2. Café discussions point to potentially more valuable problems: stock consistency, perishable-food waste, staff reliability and owner overload. These need direct validation.
3. Shift handover is an established software category. A hotel worker described notes buried in an existing PMS and guest requests being forgotten between shifts.
4. A standalone handover app still faces app-fatigue and adoption risks; a simpler workflow must prove it fits existing habits.
5. Neither idea has demonstrated willingness to pay.

## Next validation

- Compare café stock/waste, onboarding and handover problems against ShiftBrief's narrow handover workflow.
- Design a low-cost discovery test: 5–8 café owners/managers and 5–8 shift leads.
- Ask people to demonstrate current workflows and quantify frequency, time, errors, waste/cost and current spend. Do not pitch before understanding the problem.

## Guardrails

- No paid APIs, subscriptions, servers or domains without explicit approval.
- No product publishing or commercial outreach without explicit approval.
- Treat public discussions as qualitative signals, not proof of demand.
