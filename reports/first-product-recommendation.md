# ScopeSnap — First Product Recommendation

Selected for the first prototype after comparing four opportunity areas: freelancer scope changes, micro-business invoice follow-up, wedding planning and personal finance tools.

The desk research suggests scope-change friction has a concrete financial consequence, but it does not establish market size or willingness to pay. The MVP is intentionally a browser-only calculator and summary generator. No accounts, uploads, integrations, email sending, signatures or legal interpretation.
